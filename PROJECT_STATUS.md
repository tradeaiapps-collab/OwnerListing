## Workflow (confirmed by owner, 22 Oct 2026)
1. Owner creates the listing and publishes it (agent publishing is optional, later). Owner either takes photos or appoints a photographer; uses a platform solicitor or their own; uploads their own building and pest reports or uses the platform's.
2. Leads arrive in the portal and by email to the owner.
3. Owner starts negotiation. AI auto-replies to emails using the owner's chosen default or template, within the owner's mandate. AI never accepts, never gives legal advice, never reveals the floor. AI gives the owner an applicant report and summary (lawful factors only).
4. Owner decides. The AI drafts the contract from a template; both solicitors and the buyer receive copies.
5. Everything (listing, photos, reports, messages, contracts, audit) is saved in the database for future reference.

Needed next: photographer and solicitor assignment, document storage, message log and reply templates, applicant report, contract template, retention and privacy rules.

Rules to keep: contract drafts are template-based and reviewed by solicitors before signing; applicant data stored with consent (Privacy Act); every auto-reply is logged and the owner can pause it.

## Buyer workflow (confirmed by owner, 22 Oct 2026)
1. Buyer creates an account and portal, searches listings.
2. AI helps with the buyer's financial situation and gives a buying blueprint/strategy (first home buyer or investor), suggests suburb and property type, and introduces a broker from the platform.
3. Buyer details (including finances) are saved with consent.
4. Saved search with auto-notifications by email or text, per the buyer's preference, when a matching property is listed.

Rules to keep: AI gives general information and estimates, not personal financial or credit advice; broker introductions disclose any referral arrangement; suburb suggestions use budget, commute, growth and amenities, never demographics; email and SMS need consent and an unsubscribe (Spam Act 2003); financial data is minimised, encrypted and deletable (Privacy Act). Counsel to confirm credit and financial-advice licensing before launch.

## Trust account verification (decided 22 Oct 2026)
Platform holds no money; deposits go to the nominated solicitor's trust account. Nothing publishes without an approved trust account for that job (fail closed). Automated per job: practising certificate, email confirmation from the registry-listed address, account name check, bank letter on file. Call-back only if the name check is unavailable. Registered firms with unchanged, previously human-approved details are auto-approved; new firms, unregistered firms and any changed details need one person to approve. Name mismatch or expired certificate is rejected. Built: src/domain/trust-account.ts, db/006, publish gate in decidePublish, and the verification runner (src/domain/trust-verification.ts) that runs the checks through swappable services and opens an admin case when a person must act. Counsel questions: docs/COUNSEL_QUESTIONS.md. Open: the services that gather the evidence (registry lookups differ by state and may not all be automatable), encryption of account numbers, counsel review.

## Offers (decided 23 Oct 2026)
Offers on the platform only count when made through the formal form. If a buyer mentions an offer in chat, an automated message (not more than once an hour) explains that chat offers are not formal and gives a secure link to the offer form, asking whether it is their formal, final offer. ## Interim identity check (decided 23 Oct 2026)
Until an outside provider is chosen, identity checks are done by our own reviewers (src/domain/id-verification.ts, db/015). The buyer submits the front and back of a licence (or the passport photo page), the expiry date and a live selfie taken in the app. A reviewer with the new identity.review permission (compliance, super admin) ticks four checks: looks genuine, photo matches selfie, name matches the account, in date; all must pass. Australian documents need one reviewer, overseas documents two different reviewers. Staff cannot review their own check. Document numbers are never stored; images sit in private encrypted storage, every view is logged, and images are deleted 7 days after approval or 30 after rejection (counsel to confirm). Three attempts a day. Verified buyers are marked method = manual_review so they can be re-checked later by a provider. Limits: manual review cannot check government records or reliably catch deepfakes and does not scale to large volumes, so it is a stopgap. The provider interface (IdProviderPort) is ready to replace it. Finding (23 Oct): Queensland's Transport and Main Roads has only a public single-lookup page, Check driver licence status (licence number, name and date of birth; returns whether the licence is current; no class or conditions). No API or bulk access was found, and the page is for a licence holder or someone shown the licence, so automated scraping is not appropriate. The route that covers every state is the Document Verification Service through an approved provider. Option: add a reviewer checklist item to use each state's official status page by hand where one exists. Built (25 Oct 2026, PR feat/id-images, security review needed): buyer upload on /buyer/verification (versioned privacy consent recorded first; Australian ID only; camera capture inputs), type from bytes (JPEG/PNG/WebP only, HEIC refused), 10 MB cap, EXIF/XMP/text metadata stripped losslessly, ClamAV scan, AES-256-GCM per image (ID_IMAGE_KEY, AAD bound to submission, part and key), random keys, SHA-256 checked on view; reviewer claims a check for 30 minutes and only the claim holder can stream images (no-store, every view in id_review_access); approval needs the claim and every image opened; retention job POST /api/internal/id-images/retention (ID_RETENTION_CRON_SECRET) deletes images 7/30 days after the decision ("needs more" counts as rejection); append-only id_image_consent and id_submission_log (db/20261025120000). Availability is derived from config (no constant). Open: counsel on holding ID images and biometrics, pen test, pending submissions never expire.

- `/staff/automation` (permission `automation.manage`, super admin): rules "when X, tell Y" and scheduled tasks. Triggers: offer received, listing live N days, buyer views a listing N times (new `listing_view`, one counted view per hour, recorded in GET /api/listings/[id] for signed-in non-owners). Recipients: owner, appointed agent, buyer. Rules send notifications via `notifyUser` (kind `system_notice`, or `marketing_news` for opted-in buyers), so each person's channel choices apply. One `automation_run` per rule and event makes repeats harmless.
- `/staff/campaigns` (permission `campaign.manage`, super admin): email or SMS to all agents, owners, buyers, or buyers whose saved searches cover a state or suburbs. Only people opted in to marketing on that channel are reached (checked again at send time); email carries the one-click unsubscribe link and an open pixel plus tracked link; SMS goes through Twilio with the opt-out wording and is logged in `sms_message`. Recipients are fixed when sending starts; 100 messages per campaign per tick; scheduled sends start at the next tick.
- Scheduled tasks: archive draft/withdrawn listings idle N days, invite reminders (reuses `runInviteReminders`), emailed platform summary. Daily/weekly/monthly/once, Brisbane time; claimed before running; "run now" does not move the schedule.
- One tick: `POST /api/internal/staff-automation/run` (`STAFF_AUTOMATION_CRON_SECRET`), workflow `staff-automation.yml` every 15 minutes. Migration 20261105180000_automation.
- Behaviour change: marketing now allows an SMS opt-in in notification settings (needed for SMS campaigns); no notification kind is texted as marketing and Elena never suggests it.
- Open: open rates undercount where mail apps block images; the area segment uses saved searches (state, suburbs) only; no per-campaign unsubscribe-by-SMS-STOP beyond the existing STOP handling; rules cannot yet trigger on listing status changes or send to staff.
- Calendar import (PR: portal-calendar-import): Airbnb/VRBO/Booking.com iCal links imported hourly into syndication_booking (source 'ical'), clashes flagged, shown on the listing syndication page and agent workspace. Needs no partner API access. Imported blocks are not echoed on our own iCal feed (avoids loops between portals).

## Lead ads (5 Nov 2026)
Leads from Facebook/Instagram Lead Ads (webhook `/api/webhooks/ad-leads/meta`, signed with META_APP_SECRET, lead fetched with the Page token) and Google lead forms (`/api/webhooks/ad-leads/google/{sourceId}`, per-source key stored as a hash). Staff inbox at `/staff/ad-leads` (permission `ad_leads.manage`, super admin only, sensitive): assign to an active agent (agent emailed), status new/contacted/qualified/lost, enquiry and offer derived from an account with the lead's email made after the lead arrived. Spend is entered by staff per day; cost per lead, contact/enquiry/offer rates per source. Optional one-off follow-up email per source (agent contact, or up to 3 matching published listings). Provider test leads are stored but left out of figures and never emailed.
