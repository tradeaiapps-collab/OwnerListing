**Full description (4000 max)**
OwnerListing is the Australian marketplace where owners list their own homes and buyers deal with them directly.

For owners
- Create a listing step by step, with photos, floor plan and an AI helper, Elena, who guides you and answers questions.
- Choose optional helpers such as agents, solicitors and contractors. Nothing is forced on you.
- Get messages, enquiries, inspection bookings and offers in one place.
- You decide on every offer. The AI never accepts or rejects one for you.
- Deposits go to the solicitor's trust account. OwnerListing never holds your money.

For buyers
- Search homes by suburb, price and features, save favourites and compare.
- Contact owners directly and book inspections.
- Get alerts for new listings and replies.

Safe by design: identity checks, two-step sign-in, and a permanent record of messages and published files.

Note: OwnerListing is not a real estate agency and does not give legal or financial advice.
