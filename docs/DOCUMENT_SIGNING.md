# Document templates and e-signatures

Built 5 Nov 2026 (db/20261105100000). Pages: `/staff/doc-templates` (staff), `/listings/[id]/signing` (owner), `/signing` and `/signing/[id]` (signers).

- **Templates** (`src/domain/doc-templates.ts`): ordered clauses of plain text with `{{fields}}`, plus a plain-English explanation per clause. Insert-only versions; the live version is the latest publish row. A different staff person must publish (permissions `doc_template.edit`, `doc_template.publish`). Four starter templates (`src/config/document-templates.ts`) install as drafts only. Counsel must review the wording before anything is published. No contract of sale: those are state specific and prepared by the owner's solicitor.
- **Generate** (`src/domain/signing-service.ts`): the listing's owner fills the fields and names signers (each needs an active account, matched by email). The rendered text and its SHA-256 are frozen at creation (database triggers refuse changes). Send, then signers sign; to change anything, cancel and generate again.
- **Sign**: built-in click-to-sign. Signer is signed in, opens the document (recorded), types their name exactly as on their account, ticks consent. Each step is a row in `signing_event` (insert-only) with the document hash, time, IP and browser. All signers signed gives `completed`; a decline closes the document.
- **Elena**: on the signing page she explains any clause or walks through all of them, reading the staff-approved explanation aloud (browser voice). She never signs, never edits text, and every explanation is logged. No free-form AI answers on this page by design.
- **DocuSign or similar**: not connected. The service is provider-neutral at the repository boundary; a hosted provider would replace the sign step and needs an account and counsel's view on Electronic Transactions Act equivalence per state.
- **Open**: counsel sign-off on wording and on the click-to-sign approach; security review (signing is sensitive); email notification to signers (no outbound email wired for this yet, they see it at `/signing`); PDF export of the signed document and audit trail; agents generating on an owner's behalf.
