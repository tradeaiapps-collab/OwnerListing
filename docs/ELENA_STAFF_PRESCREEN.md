# Elena staff pre-screening

Elena pre-screens three staff queues so staff spend their time on unclear cases. She only recommends. Staff make every decision with the existing decision buttons; nothing is hidden, suspended or verified by Elena.

- **Rules** (pure, no model, no outside call, so no personal data leaves the server): `src/domain/elena-prescreen.ts`. Each recommendation has an action, a confidence (high/medium/low), a rule id and a reason that quotes the matched text or field.
- **Storage and data gathering**: `src/server/elena-prescreen-db.ts`; migration `db/20261120170000_elena_staff_prescreen.sql` (`elena_staff_recommendation`, `elena_staff_outcome`, both permanent).
- **Moderation** (`/staff/review-queue`): approve / hide / suspend / look yourself. Cites matched abuse words or contact details; suspend only for repeat offenders, never above medium confidence; duplicates (same account, same text) are grouped under the oldest item. A flag Elena cannot re-check (anything but a spam-only flag whose text no longer matches) is never cleared.
- **Contractor verification** (`/staff/contractor-trust`): ABN checksum, ABN used by another account, insurance and licence expired or within 45 days, licence missing for inspector and pest control, placeholder policy or licence numbers, business name vs account name. Needs-info comes with a drafted request (fits the 500-character note) that staff can use as their reject note. She cannot check the ABN register or the certificate itself and says so.
- **Complaints** (same page, Owner reports): summary of the job and the contractor's history plus suggested next steps. Suspension is suggested at medium confidence at most.
- **Logging**: each recommendation shown is stored once per rule/action/confidence (verification: per submission). After staff decide, the outcome (agreed, overrode, or no call) is stored and an audit event `elena_staff_prescreen.outcome` or `.override` is written. Logging runs after the decision and never blocks it. A failed log is invisible to staff, so check for gaps with the accuracy report.
- **Accuracy review**: `GET /api/staff/elena-prescreen/accuracy?days=30` (senior reviewers) returns agree/override counts per queue, rule and confidence.
- **Filter and bulk confirm** (off by default): checkbox "Show only what Elena recommends approving" on the moderation and verification screens. With it on, staff can tick a confirmation and approve the high-confidence items together (`POST /api/staff/elena-prescreen/bulk`). The server recomputes each recommendation, skips anything not "approve" at high confidence, applies the normal decision under the staff member's own permission and own-content block, and logs it as a bulk outcome. Limits: 25 items per call, 100 per staff member per hour. Complaints have no bulk path.
- **Verification outcome mapping**: Reject with a note counts as "needs more info".
